ABOUT SET JOBS

A recruitment partner built around people.

Established on May 6th, 2021, SET Jobs was founded with a mission to streamline recruitment and connect passionate job seekers with growth-oriented organizations.

Headquartered in Hubli, Karnataka, SET Jobs has grown into a trusted recruitment partner across multiple industries, guided by a focus on integrity, speed, and precision in staffing.
